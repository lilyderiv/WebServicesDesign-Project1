My Favourite Cities

For a glimpse into the places, here are some of my favourite cities I've been to!

Dresden, Germany

Dresden's history truly surprised me. I learned that Dresden was once known as the City of Jewels. The jewelry produced there in the past was famous and highly sought after. During our visit to Dresden, we couldn't miss the Royal Palace. Inside the palace, we observed jewelry from that era, as well as fashion items, clothing, and accessories that reflected the style of the time.

I took a photo of a rare piece of jewelry displayed there — a photo I still treasure. When I first saw it, my eyes were dazzled by its sparkle like never before. I was so captivated by its brilliance that I can only try to describe how enchanted I felt. However, I didn’t know the story behind this gem at the time. Later, I found out that it is the only diamond known to emit radiation.

Vienna, Austria

The moment I stepped into Vienna, I felt as if I had walked straight into history. The streets were so orderly, the buildings so elegant — it felt like I was staring at a painting at every corner. The place that impressed me the most was Hofsburg Palace. As I walked through its gardens, my eyes were drawn not only to nature but also to the traces of the past. In that moment, I realized: this city truly was the heart of an empire.

Budapest, Hungary

We arrived in Budapest at night. Looking out from the bus window, the shimmering lights along the Danube River immediately caught my eye. The city's silhouette at night was simply mesmerizing. During the day, the grandeur of the Parliament Building stood out; at night, it was the reflections under the bridges that won my heart. It felt like every street told its own fairy tale. Whenever I sat on a bench or paused while walking by the river, I found myself admiring the silent yet powerful stance of the surrounding architecture. To me, Budapest is a city that speaks through its buildings.

Paris, France

Paris is so hard to describe — I don’t even know where to begin. Should I start with the Eiffel Tower? The enormous halls of the Louvre? Or maybe just the feelings I had while walking along the street with my friends? This city filled me with both admiration and peace. Especially in the mornings, when the air was still crisp and the streets not yet crowded, walking through Paris felt like a privilege. I felt like I was part of a movie.

Out of all the cities I’ve seen, Paris is the one that stayed with me the most. It felt like I am watching someone I admire the most.
